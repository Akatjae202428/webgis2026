Source: mtdukes/nc-covid-by-zip, captured from N.C. DHHS and archived by WRAL. Blank counts are kept as missing when the source suppresses small values. The map uses simplified October 2, 2020 ZIP boundaries for every selected date. Data loads from GitHub, and map tiles are provided by OpenStreetMap; internet access is required. County outline source: NCDIT NC_Boundaries county layer. Local Moran I uses case counts with queen-contiguity neighbors, row-standardized weights, 999 conditional permutations, and Benjamini-Hochberg FDR correction at q <= 0.05; source-suppressed case values are excluded. Counts are not population-adjusted rates.
